MIND THE DROP

Delete your game data

On your device

Open Options → Advanced → Reset all progress in Mind the Drop to clear your local game progress. Open Diagnostics → Clear log to remove the local error log. Delete any save files you exported separately.

Google Play Games

First turn off automatic backups in Mind the Drop under Options → Cloud saves, and close the game. In Google Play Games, open Settings, then “Delete Play Games account & data”. Under individual game data, find Mind the Drop and choose Delete. This removes this game’s Play Games data, including achievements and saved progress. It does not delete your Google account or the save still on your phone. If the menu differs on your device, follow Google’s current instructions for deleting game data.

Manage automatic sign-in in Google Play Games if you want to keep playing without syncing achievements. Re-enabling backup can upload your local progress again. Google handles deletion and any retention needed to operate its services under its privacy policy; we do not keep a separate server copy of your cloud save.
